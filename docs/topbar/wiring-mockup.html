MacroTech top bar

Six options: 6 px grey wires with rounded corners, at most three vias and one red wire each. Shuffle gives all six new layouts. The framed MACROTECH from version C on today's grey bar, with PCB traces and vias as a texture behind it. The wiring is a crop of a much larger board: wires come in, turn at 45° and leave, never crossing, so you rarely see a whole one. Where an end does show, it's a via.
